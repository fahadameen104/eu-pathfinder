Privacy and contact information

EU Pathfinder uses the details you submit to respond to your enquiry. Contact info@eupathfinder.agency with questions about your information or requests to access, correct or delete enquiry details. Do not send identity documents with a request; the team can discuss an appropriate way to verify it.

Enquiry information

The form collects your name, email, nationality, chosen topic, page context and optional message, together with your permission to reply. It sends these details through Cloudflare Pages and Resend to our email inbox. Requests to the website also pass through Cloudflare, which may process connection and security information. Do not submit passport scans, payment details or sensitive documents.

Optional Google Analytics

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 uses cookies to measure visits, traffic sources and interactions such as page views, scrolls, WhatsApp or call-link clicks and a successful enquiry event. Contact clicks measure intent, not a completed conversation. Contact numbers, WhatsApp message text and enquiry field values are not included in our custom events. We do not send enquiry field values to Google Analytics. Google receives visit and device information and cookie identifiers. The Google tag is not loaded until you accept. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled.

You can reject analytics without affecting enquiries, or change your choice using . Your choice is remembered for up to 180 days; Google Analytics cookies are configured for up to 180 days. Withdrawing stops further GA4 collection and clears its first-party analytics cookies in this browser; it does not delete previously collected information. Read how Google uses information from sites using its services.

Advertising and external services

Advertising scripts remain disabled. Cloudflare also provides site analytics independently of this optional Google Analytics choice. Images may load from Unsplash, Wikimedia and FlagCDN; your browser connects to those providers when loading their resources. Opening WhatsApp or email links uses the service or app you choose.

Retention and further questions

For questions about how long enquiry details are kept, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact the team before submitting if this affects your decision.

Before agreeing to a service

Contact us for current pricing and rates. Request a written service scope, itemized fees, third-party costs, cancellation and refund terms, and complaint contact before agreeing or paying. No job, permit or visa outcome is guaranteed.
