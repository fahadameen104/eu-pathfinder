Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The enquiry form works without accepting optional analytics or advertising. The website remembers your analytics and advertising choices separately for up to 180 days.

If you accept Google Analytics, GA4 measures visits, traffic sources and interactions such as page views, scrolls, call or WhatsApp link clicks and successful enquiry events. Contact clicks indicate intent, not a completed conversation. Our custom events do not include enquiry field values, contact numbers or WhatsApp message text. Google Signals and advertising personalisation are disabled.

The Google tag is not loaded until you accept analytics. Rejecting analytics does not affect enquiries. Withdrawing stops further GA4 collection and clears its first-party cookies in this browser; it does not delete information already collected.

Advertising has a separate choice. Adsterra delivery is currently paused, and AdSense ad serving is disabled while approval and consent setup remain pending. If advertising is enabled later, review the notice and choice before accepting it.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.
