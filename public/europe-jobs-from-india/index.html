Start with a real role and destination

Work-permit rules are set by each destination country. The available route depends on the role, employer, your experience and the rules in force when you apply. Use our destination guides as a starting point, then confirm current requirements with the relevant official authorities.

  • Compare destinations and the sectors described in each country guide.
  • Check that an opportunity names a verifiable employer and a specific role.
  • Ask for the full service fees and written terms before agreeing or paying.
  • Confirm visa and work-permit requirements through official government or embassy sources.

Applying from India

Your nationality and your current country of residence can both matter. If you live in a different country from the one that issued your passport, check with the destination country's embassy or official visa service where you are allowed to submit an application. Do this before making travel plans or paying anyone.

If you live in India, distinguish the employer's recruitment process from the destination country's work-permit and visa process. Verify the named employer, role and application instructions independently.

How EU Pathfinder can help you plan

We can discuss your work history and goals, review the documents you provide, and explain the support options and fees before you decide whether to proceed. The steps below are a general overview; the process differs by country and case.

  1. Consultation and agreement

    We learn about your skills and goals, review your eligibility and agree on the service together.

  2. Document collection and permit filing

    We help you gather and check your documents, then support the work permit application.

  3. Work permit decision

    The authorities review the application. We keep you informed at each stage.

  4. Embassy appointment

    We guide you through the embassy appointment and what to prepare.

  5. Visa support and processing

    We explain the visa requirements and support you while the application is processed.

  6. Visa decision and travel preparation

    Once the visa is issued, we help you prepare your travel and relocation checklist.

  7. Arrival and settling in

    We help with accommodation and local registration so you can start your job with confidence.

Check an opportunity before you commit

A genuine recruitment process should identify the employer, role, pay, location and written terms. Verify these details independently and be cautious of anyone promising a guaranteed job, visa or work permit.

Documents to prepare for an initial discussion

These are useful starting documents, not a country-specific visa checklist. An employer or authority may request different items.

  • Valid passport
  • Updated CV
  • Educational and professional certificates
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Proof of previous work experience
  • Medical documents, where required

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for European work from India?

That depends on the destination, role, employer and current immigration rules. Review the country guide and verify the official requirements for your situation before making a decision.

Can EU Pathfinder guarantee a job, visa or work permit?

No. Employers make hiring decisions, and work-permit and visa decisions belong to the relevant authorities. Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed approval.

How can I check the fees before I start?

Ask us for the current service scope, full fee breakdown and written terms before agreeing to any service.
