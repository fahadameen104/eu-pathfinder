Refund & Cancellation Policy

Service Nature

EU PathFinder Agency provides consultancy, advisory, administrative support, document preparation assistance, and referral services. Payment for services does not constitute payment for a guaranteed outcome.

Cancellation Requests

Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to:

Written cancellation requests: Eupathfinder@gmail.com

Support Team: info@eupathfinder.agency

Refund Eligibility

All fees are earned progressively as services are performed. Refund requests will be evaluated individually and subject to:

  • Applicable consumer protection laws
  • Mandatory legal rights
  • Services completed
  • Work performed
  • Costs incurred

Generally Non-Refundable Items

Unless otherwise required by law, the following are generally non-refundable:

  • Consultation fees
  • Assessment fees
  • Administrative fees
  • Processing fees
  • Documentation review fees
  • Application preparation fees
  • Government fees
  • Embassy fees
  • Translation fees
  • Courier fees
  • Third-party expenses

Refused Applications

Refusal of a visa, permit, employment opportunity, educational admission, or immigration application does not automatically create entitlement to a refund. Refund determinations are based upon services performed rather than outcomes achieved.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds will generally be returned through the original payment method. Processing times vary according to financial institutions and payment providers.

Refund Complaints

For refund disputes, call the number below.

Written cancellation requests: Eupathfinder@gmail.com

Refunds and complaints (calls only): +1 289 914 3132

No Guarantee of Results

EU PathFinder Agency does not guarantee and expressly disclaims responsibility for any particular outcome, including but not limited to:

  • Visa approvals
  • Work permit approvals
  • Residence permit approvals
  • Citizenship approvals
  • Immigration approvals
  • Employment offers
  • Recruitment outcomes
  • Interview invitations
  • Educational admissions
  • Processing times
  • Government decisions
  • Third-party decisions

All decisions regarding visas, immigration applications, employment opportunities, educational admissions, citizenship applications, and permits are made solely by the relevant authorities, employers, institutions, or third parties.

Past results, testimonials, success rates, or previous outcomes do not guarantee future success.

Force Majeure

EU PathFinder Agency shall not be liable for delays or failures caused by circumstances beyond our reasonable control, including:

  • Government actions
  • Regulatory changes
  • Embassy closures
  • Consulate closures
  • Natural disasters
  • Public health emergencies
  • Armed conflicts
  • Cyber incidents
  • Telecommunications failures
  • Internet outages
  • Labour disputes
  • System failures

Service timelines may be extended accordingly.

Governing Law and Severability

This Privacy Policy, GDPR Notice, and Refund & Cancellation Policy shall be governed by the laws applicable in the jurisdiction from which EU PathFinder Agency operates.

If any provision of this policy is held invalid, unlawful, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

Failure by EU PathFinder Agency to enforce any provision shall not constitute a waiver of any right or provision.
