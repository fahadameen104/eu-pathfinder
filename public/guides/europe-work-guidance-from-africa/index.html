Europe Work Guidance for African Applicants

Considering work in Europe from Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Ethiopia, Egypt or South Africa? Start with your occupation, nationality, current residence and a specific destination. This guide helps prepare an enquiry; it does not advertise vacancies or confirm eligibility.

Choose a destination and verify the employer

Compare the destination guides, identify the legal employer and exact role, and check the current route with the destination government or embassy. There is no single work-permit process for every European destination.

  • Compare European destinations

Nigeria: explain your employment history

List employment, self-employment and practical experience separately, including dates, duties and references you can verify. Ask the employer what evidence it accepts before paying for additional certificates.

Ghana: describe your trade and training

Explain the tasks you perform, equipment you use and training you have completed. A certificate and a job offer are different documents; confirm what qualifications the destination employer requires.

Kenya: check occupation requirements

Identify the exact occupation before comparing destinations. If a role requires professional recognition, ask the relevant destination authority about that process before assuming experience alone is sufficient.

Uganda: check the employer and intermediary separately

Request the employer name, workplace and written job terms. Verify independently who is providing each service, what is included and who receives each payment.

Tanzania: plan document language and consistency

Check names and dates across your CV and supporting records. Ask the destination authority whether translations or certification are needed before arranging them; requirements depend on the destination.

Ethiopia: explain differences in your records

Use consistent names and dates in your enquiry. If documents use different spellings, explain the difference and ask the receiving employer or authority how to document it. Do not alter official records yourself.

Egypt: compare the offer with total costs

Request salary, hours, deductions, accommodation arrangements and the work location in writing. Separate employer terms from adviser fees, travel and application costs when comparing a proposal.

South Africa: distinguish nationality and residence

Tell the adviser both your nationality and where you currently live. Residence in South Africa and holding a South African passport are different circumstances; verify the correct application location with the destination authority.

African nationals living in the Gulf

Make your current country of residence clear when enquiring. Do not assume that another person's route or application location applies to your circumstances.

  • Planning from Gulf countries

Make a destination comparison you can verify

  • Compare one or two destinations for your actual occupation, rather than assuming every European country has the same route.
  • Record the named employer, workplace, qualifications requested, language expectations and official application source for each option.
  • Keep adviser charges, official charges, travel and living costs separate. Ask who receives each payment and what the written refund terms cover.
  • Check departure or recruitment requirements with your own country's competent authority. This guide does not replace a national clearance checklist.

Prepare evidence without oversharing

Create a CV with dates, duties, training and languages. Note which references or certificates you can obtain, but ask the employer or authority what is required before paying for new documents.

For the first discussion, share a summary of your experience and current residence. Passport scans, bank details and payment credentials are not needed in an initial enquiry. Ask how any later document request will be handled before sending files.

Prepare your first enquiry

  • Share your nationality, current residence, occupation, experience and preferred destination.
  • Ask for current rates, a written service scope and itemized costs.
  • Do not include passport scans, payment credentials or sensitive documents in an initial message.
  • Contact EU PathFinder Agency

Your preparation checklist

Use this before your first conversation. It is a planning aid, not a visa application checklist.

  • Note your nationality, current residence and one or two destinations.
  • Summarise your occupation, experience, training and languages.
  • Write down questions about the employer, role, pay and total costs.
  • Identify the official authority for your destination and any departure requirements.
  • Request the service scope and current fees in writing. Keep sensitive documents out of your initial message.
