Work routes

Routes depend on Estonia's current rules, which we confirm with you at consultation. Commonly, these include:

  • Employer-sponsored work permits
  • Seasonal or temporary work, where the destination offers it
  • Skilled-worker and shortage-occupation routes, where they exist

Sectors with regular hiring

  • Manufacturing
  • Logistics
  • Construction
  • Hospitality
  • Technology services

Demand changes with the season and with employers. We confirm what is available for your profile when you contact us.

Commonly requested documents

  • Valid passport
  • Updated CV
  • Educational and professional certificates
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Proof of previous work experience
  • Medical documents, where required

Exact requirements differ by role and by embassy. We provide a personal checklist once your case is reviewed.

How the process works

  1. Consultation and agreement

    We learn about your skills and goals, review your eligibility and agree on the service together.

  2. Document collection and permit filing

    We help you gather and check your documents, then support the work permit application.

  3. Work permit decision

    The authorities review the application. We keep you informed at each stage.

  4. Embassy appointment

    We guide you through the embassy appointment and what to prepare.

  5. Visa support and processing

    We explain the visa requirements and support you while the application is processed.

  6. Visa decision and travel preparation

    Once the visa is issued, we help you prepare your travel and relocation checklist.

  7. Arrival and settling in

    We help with accommodation and local registration so you can start your job with confidence.
