Where GDPR applies

This notice explains our data-protection commitments and how to contact us. It is not a certification or a claim that GDPR applies to every interaction. The legal rights and obligations depend on the applicable law and circumstances.

Contact info@eupathfinder.agency to ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection or withdrawal of consent. Do not send passport scans or sensitive records with an initial request.

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, including under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process personal information on one or more of the following legal bases:

Consent: where you voluntarily provide consent.

Contractual Necessity: to provide requested services.

Legal Obligations: to comply with regulatory, legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

Legitimate Interests: to improve services, maintain security, prevent fraud, and operate our business efficiently.

Vital Interests: where processing is necessary to protect an individual's rights, health, or safety.

International Data Transfers

Due to the international nature of immigration and relocation services, personal information may be processed, stored, or transferred across international borders.

Where required by applicable law, reasonable safeguards will be implemented to protect such information during cross-border transfers.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal information. Such measures may include:

  • Access controls
  • Password protection
  • Encryption technologies
  • Secure servers
  • Monitoring procedures
  • Security assessments

However, no method of online transmission, storage, or electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, EU PathFinder Agency cannot warrant or guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably required to:

  • Provide services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Protect legitimate business interests
  • Maintain records

Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, information may be securely deleted, anonymised, archived, or destroyed.

Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have the right to:

Right of Access: request access to personal information.

Right to Rectification: request correction of inaccurate information.

Right to Erasure: request deletion of information where legally permitted.

Right to Restrict Processing: request limitation of processing activities.

Right to Data Portability: request a copy of personal data in a portable format.

Right to Object: object to specific processing activities.

Right to Withdraw Consent: withdraw consent where processing is based upon consent.

Right to Lodge a Complaint: file a complaint with an appropriate supervisory authority.

Requests may be submitted to: info@eupathfinder.agency

GDPR-Specific Provisions

Where GDPR applies:

Personal information will be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently.

Only information reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes will be collected.

Data minimisation principles will be applied where appropriate.

Appropriate safeguards will be implemented to protect personal information.

Withdrawal of Consent

Where processing relies upon consent, consent may be withdrawn at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing conducted before withdrawal.

Scope of Data Rights

Nothing in this policy should be interpreted as granting rights exceeding those provided by applicable privacy legislation. Certain rights may be restricted where legal exemptions or mandatory legal obligations apply.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for persons under eighteen (18) years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors without required legal authorisation.
