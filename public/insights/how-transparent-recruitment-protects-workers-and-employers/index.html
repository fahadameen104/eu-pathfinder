How transparent recruitment protects workers and employers

Clear responsibilities, verifiable offers and written terms support more informed hiring decisions.

What to consider

A transparent recruitment process identifies the employer, role, workplace, pay, working hours and responsibilities. Workers should understand the terms before deciding whether to proceed, and employers should be clear about their requirements.

Hiring, administrative support and immigration decisions may involve different organisations. Ask who performs each service, who receives each payment and which authority decides the application.

EU PathFinder Agency’s guidance and employer-matching support should be described in writing. Any recruitment or placement service needs a separate written agreement; an enquiry or payment does not guarantee a job, permit or visa.

Your preparation checklist

  • Ask for a written service scope.
  • Separate employer terms from adviser fees.
  • Use independent official sources for current requirements.

Check the official information

  • European Union: immigration information

For a particular application, follow the destination government or embassy’s current instructions. Rules, eligibility and application locations depend on your circumstances. For non-EU destinations, use that country’s own immigration authority.

Turn your questions into a plan

Tell us your nationality, current residence, occupation, experience and the destination you are considering. Keep sensitive documents out of the initial enquiry.
