Guidance, employer matching and relocation support

Prepare your next step from India, Bangladesh, Nepal or Pakistan. Understand what support is included, what you need to provide and what happens next.

Start with an agreed scope

EU PathFinder Agency provides guidance and employer-matching support. Recruitment or placement services apply only where expressly covered by a separate written agreement. Employers make hiring decisions; authorities decide visas, work permits and residence permissions.

01

Third-country recruitment guidance

Prepare for an employer-led recruitment process from India, Bangladesh, Nepal or Pakistan. Any recruitment or placement service must be expressly covered by a separate written agreement.

Support to discuss
A discussion of the employer requirements, candidate profile and responsibilities within the agreed service scope.
What you provide
Your nationality, current residence, occupation, experience and preferred destination; employers should describe the role and workplace.
What happens next
Confirm the named employer, the service provider responsible for each stage and written terms before proceeding.
02

International job-placement preparation

Understand a prospective role before committing. Review the job requirements, written employment terms and questions to put to the employer.

Support to discuss
Support preparing a work profile and identifying questions about a specific opportunity; placement is conditional on the written agreement.
What you provide
A CV, a summary of relevant experience and details of any named employer or written offer.
What happens next
Verify the employer independently and ask who makes hiring decisions and who is responsible for each application.
03

Work visa assistance

Get administrative guidance on the destination-specific application process. The relevant authority makes all visa decisions.

Support to discuss
A preparation discussion covering the official checklist, supporting records and employer-related paperwork within the agreed scope.
What you provide
The destination, intended role, current residence and the official instructions you have received.
What happens next
Confirm the current requirements, application location and who submits the application with the responsible authority.
04

Residence permit support

Plan questions about permission to live and work in the destination, including registration, validity and renewal where relevant.

Support to discuss
Document and appointment preparation guidance for the specific residence process covered in your agreement.
What you provide
Your intended destination, nationality, current residence and any existing permit details requested through an agreed secure channel.
What happens next
Verify the applicable procedure and deadlines directly with the destination authority.
05

Candidate screening and verification preparation

Present qualifications, practical experience and supporting records clearly so an employer can assess the candidate against the role.

Support to discuss
An agreed review of the work profile and supporting information, with questions about any missing or inconsistent details.
What you provide
An accurate CV, employment dates, training details and references you can independently verify.
What happens next
Confirm which checks the employer or issuing authority will complete. Do not alter official records to resolve a discrepancy.
06

Employer-matching support

Discuss how a candidate’s skills, experience and goals align with an employer’s stated requirements. No match or offer is guaranteed.

Support to discuss
A comparison of the candidate profile and role requirements within the agreed scope.
What you provide
Your occupation, actual duties, skills, languages, experience and preferred destinations; employers provide a clear job description.
What happens next
Confirm employer identity, workplace, pay, hours, deductions and written terms before accepting an offer.
07

Documentation and compliance preparation

Organise the records needed for an employment or immigration process and understand which organisation is responsible for each requirement.

Support to discuss
An agreed document checklist and administrative preparation support based on the responsible authority’s instructions.
What you provide
The current official checklist, any written offer and an accurate summary of available records.
What happens next
Check whether translation, certification or further evidence is required before paying for those services.
08

Relocation assistance

Prepare for the practical side of moving: travel, accommodation questions, local registration and workplace expectations.

Support to discuss
A preparation discussion and relocation checklist for the destination and support specifically agreed in writing.
What you provide
The confirmed destination, proposed timing and any accommodation or arrival arrangements supplied by the employer.
What happens next
Confirm costs, responsibilities and arrival instructions before making non-refundable bookings.
09

Ongoing worker support

Discuss practical questions after arrival, including communication with the employer and documentation updates covered by the service agreement.

Support to discuss
The follow-up support and communication arrangements defined in your written agreement.
What you provide
A clear description of the question and relevant non-sensitive context.
What happens next
Use the appropriate employer or official channel for decisions and ask for independent professional advice where needed.

Industries and experience to discuss

  • Construction & infrastructure
  • Manufacturing & production
  • Warehousing & logistics
  • Agriculture & farming
  • Hospitality & tourism
  • Food processing
  • Transportation & delivery
  • Maintenance & technical services
  • Cleaning & facility management
  • Healthcare support roles

Requirements, professional recognition and available routes depend on the role and destination.

Before you decide

  • Verify the named employer and written role independently.
  • Confirm the service deliverables and your own responsibilities in writing.
  • Separate adviser fees from official charges, translations, travel and other third-party costs.
  • Ask what is refundable, when charges become due and how to cancel.
