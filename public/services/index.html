Choose the support you need

Start with your situation. Confirm the scope before committing.

01

Consultation and planning

Support to discuss
Discuss your work history, goals and destinations, and identify questions to resolve before you proceed.
What you provide
A summary of your occupation, experience, nationality, current residence and preferred destinations.
Your next step
Ask which support fits your situation and request its scope and current fees in writing.
02

Document preparation guidance

Support to discuss
Discuss the documents relevant to your proposed route and the preparation support available. Confirm any review or translation arrangements in your agreement.
What you provide
An accurate CV and a list of records you already hold. Send copies only when requested through an agreed method.
Your next step
Check the receiving employer or authority's current checklist before arranging certificates, translations or medical checks.
03

Work-permit and visa process guidance

Support to discuss
Discuss the steps, responsibilities and preparation involved in the destination's application process. The agreed scope determines what assistance is included.
What you provide
The destination, any named employer or offer, and accurate information about your circumstances.
Your next step
Confirm who submits each application, which charges are separate and which official authority makes the decision.
04

Travel and relocation planning

Support to discuss
Discuss practical preparation for travel, accommodation and arrival. Confirm which arrangements, if any, the team will assist with before paying.
What you provide
Your destination, expected dates, household needs and any approvals already received.
Your next step
Check the required approvals before booking non-refundable travel, and confirm third-party costs and responsibilities.

Know what you are agreeing to

  • Request the deliverables, responsibilities and communication arrangements in writing.
  • Separate service fees from government charges, translations, travel and other third-party costs.
  • Ask about payment stages, cancellation and refund terms before paying.
  • Employers decide hiring; authorities decide permits, visas and appointments. No outcome is guaranteed.
